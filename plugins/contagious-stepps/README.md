# Contagious STEPPS Framework Plugin

A Claude Code plugin that helps you analyze and enhance the viral potential of your products, ideas, or campaigns using Jonah Berger's STEPPS framework from "Contagious: Why Things Catch On."

## What It Does

This plugin provides an AI agent skill that systematically analyzes your offering against six proven principles of virality:

- **Social Currency**: Make people look good by sharing
- **Triggers**: Top of mind, tip of tongue
- **Emotion**: When we care, we share
- **Public**: Built to show, built to grow
- **Practical Value**: News you can use
- **Stories**: Information travels under the guise of idle chatter

The skill guides you through discovering opportunities, generating enhancement ideas, and creating actionable plans to make your product or campaign more contagious.

## When to Use This Plugin

Use this plugin when you need help with:

- Increasing word-of-mouth for a product, service, or idea
- Launching a marketing campaign and maximizing organic sharing
- Making a behavior change initiative more contagious
- Analyzing why something did or didn't go viral
- Brainstorming ways to make content more shareable
- Developing viral marketing strategies
- Understanding what makes things catch on

## How It Works

The agent follows a structured protocol:

1. **Discovery**: Asks questions about your offering, target audience, and current approach
2. **STEPPS Analysis**: Systematically evaluates your offering against each principle
3. **Ideation**: Generates specific enhancement ideas for weak areas
4. **Prioritization**: Helps you choose the highest-impact initiatives
5. **Action Plan**: Creates concrete next steps with success metrics

## About the Framework

The STEPPS framework is based on Jonah Berger's research into what makes things go viral. The six principles work together to create word-of-mouth:

1. **Social Currency**: People share things that make them look good, knowledgeable, or "in-the-know"
2. **Triggers**: Frequent environmental cues that prompt discussion
3. **Emotion**: High-arousal emotions (awe, excitement, anger) drive sharing more than low-arousal ones
4. **Public**: Observable things get imitated - "built to show, built to grow"
5. **Practical Value**: Useful information that helps others gets shared readily
6. **Stories**: Information travels when woven into compelling narratives

The most contagious ideas typically leverage multiple principles simultaneously.
